About us

We're a design studio that produces original visual work at the volume AI training demands.

Our mission

To make original creative work available at industrial volume, so the companies building visual AI never have to choose between quality and scale.

What we hold on to.

It's the method that carries the standard. Quality that lives in one person's taste does not survive the thousandth piece. Ours lives in a written method: a catalogue of techniques, enforced rotation, and automated gates that run before anything leaves us. Anyone trained in it produces to the same bar.

It's the record that makes it verifiable. Every piece is logged with its technique, palette, typography and reference. We can show you the distribution of our own output instead of asking you to trust it. Most studios cannot, because most studios do not record.

It's the incentive that holds at scale. Operators are paid per approved piece and rework is our cost, not yours. That is what keeps the bar in the same place at the first piece and at the ten-thousandth.

It's the geography that makes the economics work. Price per asset is global; the cost of producing it is not. Brazil is one of the few places with both senior design density and a cost structure that makes a hundred thousand pieces viable at market rates. And unlike vendors further east, we work your hours: full workday overlap with US time zones.
